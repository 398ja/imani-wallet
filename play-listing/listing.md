## Full description (1248 / 4000)

```
Imani is a wallet for digital vouchers from local shops, cafes and market stalls.

A shop sells you a voucher and it lands in your wallet straight away. Pay with it at the counter by scanning the shop's code, or show your own code to receive one. Your balance is shown per shop, so you always know what you hold and where you can spend it.

Send to a friend
Pick a voucher, enter an amount and a short note, and send it to a friend's Imani handle. It arrives in their wallet and they can spend it at the same shop.

Cashlinks
Not everyone has Imani yet. Create a Cashlink, a one-time link or QR code that anyone can claim once before it expires. If nobody claims it in time, it comes back to you on its own.

For shops and stalls
Run a stall from the same app. Sell a voucher to a customer by scanning their code, redeem a voucher when they pay, and see what you have issued, what came back and what is still valid.

You hold your own key
Imani is non-custodial. Your vouchers and your key stay on your device. Imani cannot move or freeze them. You make your own backup, as a file or in your own Google Drive, and you can restore your wallet from it. Keep your backup safe: without it, a lost phone means lost vouchers.

Questions: hello@imani.casa
```
